= Slots and extension points

The slot index lists every place where a xref:reference/glossary.adoc#zimlet[Zimlet] can add content to the xref:reference/glossary.adoc#modern-ui[Modern UI] or take part in its processing. Each entry gives the name of the slot, the screen where it appears and what a handler for it receives and returns. Look up a slot here before you register a handler for it. xref:fundamentals/extending-the-ui.adoc[Slots and handlers] explains how a handler fills a slot.

Slots are grouped by the area of the Modern UI that shows them, and each group is split by screen. Data keys are separate because the Modern UI reads their results as data and does not draw them. To find the name of a slot on an open screen, xref:run-package-deploy/run-and-debug.adoc#debug-slots[Show every slot in the page].

[#groups]
== Groups

* xref:reference/slots/app-shell.adoc[App shell]: slots of the frame around every screen.
** xref:reference/slots/app-shell/navigation-and-tabs.adoc[Navigation and tabs]: the tab bar of an area, the list of areas and the account menu.
** Landing pages: the start page of an area.
** App frame and sign-in: app-wide content, routes, the panels at the edge of the window and the sign-in screens.
** Settings and gear menus: the gear menu and its links.
** Ad slots: places for advertisements.
** Search: the search field and web search.
* Mail: slots of the Mail area.
** Message viewer: an open message.
** Mail list and sidebar: the message list, the folder list and the sidebar.
** Mail menus and actions: the menus of messages and folders.
** Attachments: actions on attachments and the attachment viewer.
** Composer: the window where the user writes a message.
* Calendar: slots of the Calendar area.
** Calendar view: the calendar, its folder list and its subscriptions.
** Events and appointments: event menus, event details and the appointment editor.
* Contacts: slots of the Contacts area.
* Briefcase: slots of the Briefcase area.
* Settings: slots of the Settings dialog.
** General and writing: the general, writing, signature and filter pages.
** Calendar and reminders: the calendar and reminders page.
* Data keys: keys that the Modern UI reads as data, such as message processors and the settings registration.
* All slots A to Z: every name in one alphabetical list.
