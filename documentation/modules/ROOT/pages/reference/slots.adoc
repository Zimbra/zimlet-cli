= Slots and extension points

The slot index lists every place where a xref:reference/glossary.adoc#zimlet[Zimlet] can add content to the xref:reference/glossary.adoc#modern-ui[Modern UI] or take part in its processing. Each entry gives the name of the slot, the screen where it appears and what a handler for it receives and returns. Look up a slot here before you register a handler for it. xref:fundamentals/extending-the-ui.adoc[Slots and handlers] explains how a handler fills a slot.

Slots are grouped by the area of the Modern UI that shows them, and each group is split by screen. Two groups are separate because they draw nothing. The slots that hide a built-in part of the Modern UI share one handler and one rule. The content processors are separate because the Modern UI uses their results as content. To find the name of a slot on an open screen, xref:run-package-deploy/run-and-debug.adoc#debug-slots[Show every slot in the page].

[#groups]
== Groups

* xref:reference/slots/app-shell.adoc[App shell]: slots of the frame around every screen.
** xref:reference/slots/app-shell/navigation-and-tabs.adoc[Navigation and tabs]: the tab bar of a xref:reference/glossary.adoc#vertical[vertical], the list of verticals and the start page of a vertical.
** xref:reference/slots/app-shell/app-frame.adoc[App frame]: app-wide content, routes and the panels at the edge of the window.
** xref:reference/slots/app-shell/header-menus.adoc[Header menus]: the *Settings and more* menu and the account menu.
** xref:reference/slots/app-shell/ad-slots.adoc[Ad slots]: places for advertisements.
** xref:reference/slots/app-shell/search.adoc[Search]: the search field and web search.
** xref:reference/slots/app-shell/folder-list.adoc[Folder list]: the folder list that Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Briefcase share.
* xref:reference/slots/mail.adoc[Mail]: slots of the Mail vertical.
** xref:reference/slots/mail/message-viewer.adoc[Message viewer]: an open message.
** xref:reference/slots/mail/mail-list-and-sidebar.adoc[Mail list and sidebar]: the message list and the sidebar.
** xref:reference/slots/mail/mail-menus-and-actions.adoc[Mail menus and actions]: the menus of messages and folders.
** xref:reference/slots/mail/attachments.adoc[Attachments]: actions on the attachments of a message and the attachment viewer.
* xref:reference/slots/composer.adoc[Composer and editor]: the window where the user writes a message, the rich text editor and the address fields that other screens share.
** xref:reference/slots/composer/mail-composer.adoc[Mail composer]: the toolbar, the header, the sender options and the attachment menu of the composer.
** xref:reference/slots/composer/editor-and-addresses.adoc[Editor and address fields]: the rich text editor and the address fields that Mail, Calendar and other screens share.
* xref:reference/slots/calendar.adoc[Calendar]: slots of the Calendar vertical.
** xref:reference/slots/calendar/calendar-view.adoc[Calendar view]: the calendar, its folder list and its subscriptions.
** xref:reference/slots/calendar/events-and-appointments.adoc[Events and appointments]: event menus, event details and the appointment editor.
* xref:reference/slots/contacts.adoc[Contacts]: slots of the Contacts vertical.
* xref:reference/slots/briefcase.adoc[Briefcase]: slots of the Briefcase vertical.
* xref:reference/slots/settings.adoc[Settings]: slots of the Settings dialog and the key that adds a section to it.
* xref:reference/slots/hide-built-in-ui.adoc[Hide built-in UI]: slots that hide a built-in control, menu item, label or link and add nothing.
* xref:reference/slots/content-processors.adoc[Content processors]: keys that change or supply the content of a message or an event before the Modern UI shows it.
* xref:reference/slots/all-slots.adoc[All slots A to Z]: every name in one alphabetical list.
