= App shell

The app shell is the frame around every screen of the xref:reference/glossary.adoc#modern-ui[Modern UI]. It holds the navigation, the menus in the header, the panels at the edge of the window and the search field. The slots in this group are not tied to Mail, Calendar, Contacts or Briefcase. Use them when your xref:reference/glossary.adoc#zimlet[Zimlet] adds something for the whole application. Examples are a menu link, a tab, an advertisement or a feature that runs in the background. The slots that hide a built-in part, such as the *About* item or the search tips link, are in xref:reference/slots/hide-built-in-ui.adoc[Hide built-in UI].

Every slot has an entry with the place where it appears, the props its handler receives and what the handler returns. xref:fundamentals/extending-the-ui.adoc[Slots and handlers] explains how a handler fills a slot.

[#screens]
== Screens

[[navigation-and-tabs]]pass:[<a class="anchor" href="#navigation-and-tabs"></a>]xref:reference/slots/app-shell/navigation-and-tabs.adoc[Navigation and tabs]::
Add a tab or a link to a xref:reference/glossary.adoc#vertical[vertical], or to the list of verticals at the top. Replace the start page of a vertical, or add text and videos to it. Slots: `<vertical>-tab-item`, `<vertical>-default-tab`, `menu`, `vertical-menu-item`, `<vertical>-landing-page`, `<vertical>-landing-page-description` and `<vertical>-landing-page-videos`.

[[app-frame]]pass:[<a class="anchor" href="#app-frame"></a>]xref:reference/slots/app-shell/app-frame.adoc[App frame]::
Run a feature for the whole application and add screens with routes. Fill the panels at the right edge of the window. Slots: `app`, `routes`, `rightbar-50px` and `rightside-zimlet-slot`.

[[header-menus]]pass:[<a class="anchor" href="#header-menus"></a>]xref:reference/slots/app-shell/header-menus.adoc[Header menus]::
Add items to the *Settings and more* menu behind the gear icon or to the account menu, or add text to the *Change Password* dialog. Slots: `settings-dropdown-item`, `settings-dropdown-item-end`, `gear-menu`, `header-menu-item` and `change-password-description`.

[[ad-slots]]pass:[<a class="anchor" href="#ad-slots"></a>]xref:reference/slots/app-shell/ad-slots.adoc[Ad slots]::
Show advertisements beside the screens, along the bottom edge of a narrow window and above lists. Slots: `adSlot-160-600`, `adSlot-300-250-bottom`, `adSlot-300-250-top`, `adSlot-300-600`, `adSlot-adhesion-320-50`, `adSlot-adhesion-728-90`, `adSlot-left-120-90`, `adSlot-left-180-150`, `adSlot-left-300-250`, `adSlot-news`, `top-mail-ad-item` and `top-mail-generic-slot`.

[[search]]pass:[<a class="anchor" href="#search"></a>]xref:reference/slots/app-shell/search.adoc[Search]::
Change the search field or add a web search. Slots: `searchInputPlaceholder`, `web-search` and `web-search-toggle`.

[[folder-list]]pass:[<a class="anchor" href="#folder-list"></a>]xref:reference/slots/app-shell/folder-list.adoc[Folder list]::
Add content to the folder list that Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Briefcase share. Slots: `folder-group`, `folder-list-middle` and `folder-list-end`.
